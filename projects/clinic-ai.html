Clinic AI Booking Agent — 24/7 Bilingual Assistant

An intelligent conversational appointment assistant for a clinic in Egypt. Operates 24/7 in English and Egyptian Arabic, orchestrating live Google Calendar availability checks, service duration reservations, and administrative Google Sheets sync.

Client / Domain Medical Clinic (Egypt)
My Role AI Workflow Designer & Automation Developer
Tech Stack n8n, Google Gemini (LLM), Google Calendar API, Sheets API
Languages Supported English & Egyptian Arabic (Masri Dialect)

The Problem

Medical clinics face constant administrative strain managing appointment inquiries and scheduling requests. The clinic in Egypt was challenged with:

  • Missed Bookings Outside Working Hours: Prospective patients contacting the clinic at night or during weekends were lost to competitors.
  • Friction & Delays: Receptionists spent excessive time on back-and-forth phone calls and WhatsApp messages trying to find mutual availability.
  • Double Bookings: Manual coordination caused scheduling conflicts and overlapped doctor time slots.
  • Dialect Barriers: Generic chatbot solutions failed to recognize authentic Egyptian Arabic (Masri) phrases and everyday colloquial expressions.

The Solution

I designed and built an autonomous n8n workflow centered around an intelligent AI Agent node powered by Google Gemini. Patients can converse naturally in English or Egyptian Arabic to schedule, check, adjust, or cancel appointments. Before confirming, the agent performs a mandatory real-time check against the doctor's live calendar, computes exact procedure durations, and updates the clinic's master ledger in Google Sheets.

Key Features

💬 24/7 Conversational Booking

Patients chat naturally at any time of day or night to reserve appointments without requiring human receptionist intervention.

🇪🇬 Bilingual (English & Egyptian Arabic)

Fluently comprehends and replies in Egyptian Arabic (Masri dialect) as well as English, understanding local phrasing and colloquial date terms.

🔒 Mandatory Availability Guardrail

The agent is strictly prevented from committing to any booking before querying Google Calendar to verify the doctor's real-time free slots.

⏱️ Service-Specific Duration Handling

Recognizes each clinic medical service and dynamically blocks the precise required time slot (e.g. 30 min consultation vs. 60 min procedure).

🔄 Full Appointment Lifecycle

Handles complete scheduling operations: booking new visits, looking up existing bookings, updating/rescheduling dates, and managing cancellations.

📊 Real-Time Google Sheets Sync

Automatically records new bookings with patient info, updates rows on changes, and deletes records on cancellations for clinic management.
