LegalAI — Arabic Legal Case Simplifier & Client Assistant

An intelligent LegalTech assistant engineered for law firms to transform complex, archaic Arabic court notifications into concise, plain-language client reports with rigorous hallucination auditing and human-in-the-loop review.

Client / Domain Law Firms & Legal Practice (Egypt & MENA)
My Role AI Pipeline Architecture & Full-Stack Development
Tech Stack Python 3.10+, OpenAI/OpenRouter API, Arabic NLP, HTML5 UI
Key Capability Zero-Hallucination Bi-Numeral Auditing

The Problem

In modern legal practices across the Arab world, court updates and hearing proceedings are recorded in archaic, complex, and formal legal Arabic phrasing. Clients receiving these raw updates face high anxiety and confusion:

  • Archaic Procedural Jargon: Clients don't understand phrases like "تأجيل لعدم تمام الإعلان" or "ضم المفردات".
  • Lawyer Communication Fatigue: Attorneys spend dozens of billable hours every week repeatedly answering: "Did we lose?", "Do I have to attend?", and "What should I do next?".
  • High AI Hallucination Risk: Standard off-the-shelf LLMs frequently invent hearing dates, alter case numbers, or hallucinate procedural outcomes, which is catastrophic in legal settings.

The Solution

LegalAI operates as a deterministic, multi-stage paralegal communication engine. It normalizes Arabic source text, retrieves grounded definitions from a curated legal glossary, formats requests under strict JSON schema constraints, and executes an automated dual-numeral audit comparing source and generated dates and case numbers before presenting results.

Key Features

📝 Plain-Language Legal Synthesis

Produces structured summaries covering: 1) What Happened, 2) Plain Meaning, 3) Next Steps Timeline, 4) Required Client Action, and 5) Decoded Key Terms.

🔤 Custom Arabic NLP Normalizer

Strips Tashkeel diacritics, unifies letter variants (أ/إ/آ → ا, ة → ه, ى → ي), and supports prefix-tolerant matching for Arabic prepositions (بالـ, والـ, فالـ).

📚 Grounded Legal Glossary

Contextual knowledge injection mapping authentic Egyptian and Arab court procedural terminology directly into simplified 6th-grade Arabic definitions.

🔍 Bi-Numeral Hallucination Auditor

Audits output against source text by converting both Eastern Arabic (١، ٢، ٣) and Western Arabic (1, 2, 3) numerals to guarantee dates and case numbers are never fabricated.

🛡️ Human-in-the-Loop Triage

Flags ambiguous, incomplete, or high-risk case notes with an immediate needs_lawyer_review warning, preventing accidental delivery of incorrect advice.

⚡ Zero-Dependency Architecture

Engineered with Python's built-in standard library HTTP server and OpenAI SDK — eliminating heavy framework bloat and ensuring sub-second execution.
