Reviva — Sports Recovery & Massage Center Website

A professional, performance-led website for an elite sports recovery and wellness center in Athens, Greece. Designed to convert visitors into booked sessions with clear pricing, service filters, and integrated appointment scheduling.

Client / Domain Reviva Sports Recovery (Athens, Greece)
My Role Website Design, Content Architecture & Wix Setup
Platform Wix, Wix Bookings, Custom Domain & SEO
Live Production revivacenter.net

The Problem

Reviva provides specialized athletic recovery therapies (whole-body cryotherapy, targeted sports massage, compression boots, and beauty treatments) in Palaio Faliro, Athens. The center needed:

  • Frictionless Client Onboarding: A smooth digital journey allowing athletic clients to review therapy details and reserve appointments in seconds without calling.
  • Transparent Pricing: Demystifying recovery costs with upfront pricing and package options for single visits and recurring athlete programs.
  • Brand Authority: Establishing a premium, performance-oriented brand identity ("Sessions, Not Classes") distinct from standard spas.
  • Local Search Dominance: Ranking effectively for targeted recovery queries in the southern suburbs of Athens.

The Solution

I designed and launched a modern, mobile-first website on Wix configured with Wix Bookings. The site highlights Reviva's evidence-based recovery pillars, organizes therapies into clear searchable categories with transparent pricing, and features persistent booking call-to-actions that guide visitors directly to confirmed time slots.

Key Features

📅 Direct Calendar Booking Engine

Integrated scheduling system allowing clients to select services, choose therapists, view available slots, and book instantly online.

🏷️ Transparent Service & Pricing Catalog

Structured category filtering for Sports Massage, Cryotherapy, Compression Boots, Aesthetic Care, and multi-visit athlete packages.

⚡ "Sessions, Not Classes" Positioning

Clear, athlete-focused brand messaging that emphasizes customized, week-by-week treatments tailored to individual recovery requirements.

📱 Mobile-First Conversion Flow

Sticky header navigation with persistent "Book Now" buttons engineered for high conversion rates on smartphones.

📍 Local Athens SEO Optimization

Targeted metadata, location schema, and Google Maps integration focused on Palaio Faliro and Athens athletic communities.

🎁 Digital Gift Cards & Packages

Dedicated channels for purchasing treatment packages and digital gift vouchers to encourage repeat client visits.
