Headless CMS & Web Platform

Al-Hanaa Agri-Catalog — Bilingual Agrochemicals Platform

An enterprise-grade digital product showcase and technical catalog engineered for an agricultural enterprise, delivering bilingual (Arabic/English) RTL/LTR content, headless Sanity CMS authoring, and sub-second edge distribution on Cloudflare.

Client / Domain Agrochemicals & Crop Nutrition Enterprise
My Role Lead Frontend Engineering & Headless Architecture
Tech Stack Next.js 16, React 19, Sanity Studio v3, Tailwind CSS
Live Production alhanaa-agriculture.com

The Problem

Agricultural products like crop protection agents and specialized fertilizers require rigorous technical transparency — including active ingredient concentrations, application rates per hectare, and safety intervals. Traditional paper and static PDF catalogs faced severe hurdles:

  • Trapped Technical Data: Growers and agronomists couldn't easily search, filter, or reference specific formulation details from the field.
  • Bilingual Disconnect: The MENA agribusiness sector requires authentic Arabic technical terminology alongside international English nomenclature with fluid RTL/LTR support.
  • Bottlenecked Updates: Agronomists and marketing teams had to wait on software developers for simple changes to chemical dosages, product slides, or safety data sheets.
  • Slow Rural Connectivity: Farm locations frequently operate on high-latency mobile networks, necessitating instantaneous page loads.

The Solution

I engineered a decoupled, headless architecture pairing Next.js 16 Static Site Generation with Sanity.io's structured Content Lake and Cloudflare Pages edge delivery. This guarantees sub-second page delivery across the globe, zero hosting maintenance overhead, and gives the client's non-technical staff full visual control over their bilingual product catalog.

Key Features

🌐 Native Bilingual i18n & RTL

Zero-flicker layout swapping between Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) with synchronized route mirroring (e.g. /ar/products/... and /en/products/...).

🧪 Technical Dosage & Spec Sheets

Structured Portable Text tables detailing active ingredients, chemical formulations, target pests, and precise hectare application rates.

📦 Custom 2:3 Canister Packaging Layout

Tailored aspect ratio display designed specifically for agricultural bottles and canisters, complete with touch-enabled fullscreen image lightbox.

⚙️ Tailored Sanity Studio Workspace

Custom desk structure with singleton site settings, hotspot image cropping, and 3-tier hierarchical taxonomy: Category → Subcategory → Product.

⚡ Zero Server Footprint (Cloudflare Pages)

Static pre-rendering (`output: 'export'`) distributed across Cloudflare's global edge network, delivering near-instant load times even on slow 3G cellular connections.

🏆 Regulatory & Accreditations Grid

Dynamic showcase for verified international certifications, quality compliance badges, and interactive corporate values cards.
